## ✨ Features

### 📅 Smart Timetable Management
- **Visual Weekly Schedule**: Clean, intuitive weekly view of all your classes
- **Custom Time Slots**: Add, edit, and delete class periods with ease
- **Recurring Classes**: Support for weekly recurring lectures, tutorials, and labs
- **Color Coding**: Assign different colors to different subjects for quick identification
- **Conflict Detection**: Automatically alerts you if you try to double-book a time slot

### 📝 Assignment & Task Tracker
- **Deadline Management**: Track upcoming assignments, quizzes, and project deadlines
- **Priority Levels**: Mark tasks as High, Medium, or Low priority
- **Completion Status**: Check off completed tasks and track your progress
- **Push Notifications**: Get reminders before deadlines (coming soon)

### 📚 Study Tools
- **Study Session Planner**: Block out study time for specific subjects
- **Grade Calculator**: Calculate your current grade and what you need on finals
- **Notes Section**: Quick notes for each class (syllabus, professor info, etc.)

### 🎨 Beautiful UI & Customization
- **Material Design 3**: Follows latest Material You design guidelines
- **Dark/Light Mode**: Automatic theme switching based on system settings
- **Custom Accent Colors**: Personalize the app with your favorite colors
- **Week Start Selection**: Choose whether your week starts on Sunday or Monday

### 💾 Data Management
- **Local Storage**: All data stored securely on your device using Hive/SharedPreferences
- **Backup & Restore**: Export your schedule to JSON and import it later
- **No Account Required**: Full privacy - no sign-up needed

### 📱 Multi-Platform Support
- **Android**: Full support for phones and tablets
- **iOS**: Coming soon
- **Web**: Coming soon
- **Desktop**: Windows/macOS/Linux (experimental)
